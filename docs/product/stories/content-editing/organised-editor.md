# An editor organised around my work

<!-- docs/product/stories/content-editing/organised-editor.md. Story 3 of epic-content-editing. -->

As Star, I want the editor's sections to be Comics, Art, Writing, Shop, Commissions, and Site settings, with plain help text on every field, so that I find what I want to change without knowing how the site is built.

## Acceptance criteria

- The editor's main menu lists Comics, Art, Writing, Shop, Commissions, and Site settings, in that order, and nothing else.
- Under Comics, each series lists its chapters in number order, so Star can go from a series to a chapter to its pages.
- Every field has a plain-language label and help text that says what it does on the site, without technical words such as "slug", "reference", "document", or "schema".
- Fields Star must fill in say so, and the editor explains what's missing before Star can publish.
- The editor never shows developer tools (such as the query tool) to Star.
- Star tries the reorganised editor on a few real pieces, with the lead, and what they find is written down as changes to this story or new stories before the epic continues.
