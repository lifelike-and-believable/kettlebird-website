# Find and share a story

<!-- docs/product/stories/story-reader/find-and-share-a-story.md. Story 4 of epic-story-reader. -->

As a fan, I want the news page and the Writing page to take me to a story on the site, and a shared link to a story or chapter to show its title and cover, so that a story I post about on social media lands readers on Star's site.

## Acceptance criteria

- The news page links a story with its text on the site to its page; other stories keep their current link.
- A link to a story or a chapter, shared on social media, shows the story's title (and the chapter's), its short description and its cover.
- A new chapter appears on the news page when it's published, linking to that chapter.
- An address for a story or chapter that doesn't exist shows **Page not found**, with a link back to the Writing page.
