# Editing your art, writing, shop and commissions

<!-- docs/user/editing-your-site.md. For Star (persona-artist): finding your way round the editor's
menu and filling in each kind of item. Written from design-organised-editor. Comics have their own
guide (publishing-comics.md); signing in is in getting-started.md. -->

Open the editor at `kettlebird.com/studio` (signing in is in [Getting started](getting-started.md)). Its menu, on the left, has six sections, always in this order: **Comics**, **Art**, **Writing**, **Shop**, **Commissions** and **Site settings**. Choose one to see what's in it. Each section's list has a + button at the top to add something new (**New series**, **New piece**, and so on), then your items, each with a short detail to tell them apart.

Every field has a short note under its name saying what it does on the site. Fields marked **Required**, or **Choose at least one**, must be filled in before you can publish. A new item shows "Not published yet" in its list until you publish it.

In a **Description**, **Short description** or **What's included**, press Enter to start a new line, and leave a blank line between paragraphs: the site shows them as you typed them.

## Comics

Your series, newest first, each with its status. Choose a series to see its details and its **Chapters**, in number order; choose a chapter to open it and its pages beside the series, headed with both names, like "Kettle Tales · Chapter 2: The Long Road". The series stays open on the left. Everything about comics, from starting a series to uploading pages, is in [Publishing comic pages and chapters](publishing-comics.md).

## Art

**Art** has two lists: **Pieces**, your portfolio pieces, newest first, each with the portfolio sections it's in and any subfolders; and **Subfolders** (see [Subfolders](#subfolders)). In **Pieces**, choose the + button, **New piece**, to add one.

| Field | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Title** (required) | Shown when someone points at the picture, and as the heading when it's opened. |
| **Picture** (required) | Upload the original file; the site makes smaller copies. Pieces show whole; in a subfolder's grid they're cropped square. To keep the important part in view there, choose the picture's **Edit** button, then drag the circle over the part that matters. |
| **Alt text** | What the picture shows, for people using screen readers. If it's empty, they hear only the title. You can publish without it, but the field reminds you. |
| **Portfolio sections** (choose at least one) | Original Work, Fan Work, Past Clients, Featured, Work in Progress. A piece can be in more than one. The newest piece in Featured is also the picture when someone shares the Portfolio's link. |
| **Subfolders** | Optional. At most one in each section this piece is in. Left as Loose, the piece shows on its own in that section. |
| **Description** | Shown when someone opens the picture on your portfolio. |
| **Tags** | Words shown under the picture when it's opened, like a character's name. Type one, then press Enter. |
| **Date** | When you made it. The portfolio shows newest first; pieces without a date come last. |

## Subfolders

A subfolder gathers pieces inside one section of your portfolio, such as one fandom in Fan Work or one client in Past Clients. On the Portfolio it shows as one thumbnail: a grid of its newest pieces with its name under it. Choosing it shows only its pieces, the section's tab reads like "Fan Work: Murderbot", and a pink **INDEX** tab goes back to the whole section. Each subfolder has its own web address, so people can share it.

To make one:

1. In **Art**, choose **Subfolders**, then the + button.
2. Fill in its fields, then choose **Publish**. Publish the subfolder before you put pieces in it: a piece offers only published subfolders.

| Field | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Name** (required) | Shown under its thumbnail, and on the section's tab after the section's name when it's open ("Fan Work: Murderbot"). |
| **Web address** (required) | Made from the name. Leave it as it is once people may have shared links to it. |
| **Section** (required) | The Portfolio section it's in. Set when you make it; to move pieces to another section, make a subfolder there. |
| **Grid size** | How many of its pieces its thumbnail shows, newest first: 2 × 2, 3 × 3 or 4 × 4. Under it, the editor says how many pieces it has. Phones show at most 2 × 2. |

To put a piece in a subfolder, open the piece and, under **Subfolders**, choose it beside its section. A piece can be in a subfolder in one section and loose in another: a Murderbot piece can sit in Fan Work's Murderbot and loose in Featured. If you untick a section, the piece leaves its subfolder there when you publish; the editor says so first.

In the **Subfolders** list, each one reads like "Fan Work · 2 × 2 · 4 pieces". A subfolder with no published pieces doesn't show on the Portfolio.

- Renaming a subfolder changes its name everywhere; its web address stays, so shared links keep working.
- Deleting a subfolder asks first and says how many pieces it holds. They stay in the section, shown on their own, and its web address stops working.

## Writing

Your stories, newest first, each with the date it was posted. Choose the + button, **New story**, to add one. To put a story's text on the site, as a one-shot or in chapters, see [Putting stories on the site](publishing-stories.md).

| Field | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Title** (required) | The story's title. |
| **Link to read it** | Where the Writing page's Read it button goes, such as the story on AO3. |
| **Short description** | A sentence or two, shown on the Writing page. |
| **Cover** | Shown beside the story on the Writing page, cropped to a tall rectangle. |
| **Genres** | Shown as tags on the Writing page. Type one, then press Enter. |
| **Date posted** | The Writing page shows newest first. |

## Shop

Your products, by name within each shop section, as the shop lists them. Choose the + button, **New product**, to add one.

| Field | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Name** (required) | The product's name in the shop. |
| **Shop section** (required) | Which section of the shop it's listed under: Prints, Stickers, or Digital. |
| **Price** | Shown exactly as you write it, so include the currency: $4, or $4 each. |
| **Pictures** | The first is the one shown in the shop, cropped square from the centre. Drag a picture to change the order. |
| **Description** | Shown in the shop under the name. |
| **Ko-fi link** | Where the shop's Buy button goes, until the site has its own checkout. Without it, there's no Buy button. |

## Commissions

Your commission types, in the order they appear on the Commissions page. Choose the + button, **New commission type**, to add one.

| Field | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Name** (required) | Like "Full colour illustration", shown as the heading. |
| **Price range** | Shown exactly as you write it, like $80–150. |
| **What's included** | Shown under the name on the Commissions page. |
| **Examples** | Pictures of past commissions of this kind. The first three are shown, cropped square from the centre. Drag a picture to change the order. |
| **How long it takes** | Shown as "Est. delivery", like 2–3 weeks. |
| **Order on the page** | Lower numbers come first on the Commissions page, and in this list. Number them 1, 2, 3; to move one, change the numbers. |

Whether commissions are open or closed is set in **Site settings**: see [Open or close commissions](site-settings.md#open-or-close-commissions).

## Site settings

This is where you open or close commissions, and change your about page, the home page's artist text, your contact email and your social links: see [Commissions, your about page, the home page and your links](site-settings.md).

## Publish

**Publish** is at the bottom of the open item. Until you publish, your changes are saved as a draft: visitors don't see it, and the site doesn't change. After you choose **Publish**, the site updates on its own, usually within a minute or two, and the status at the top right says when your change is live: see [When your change is live](going-live.md).

To check your change on its page before you publish it, read [See it before you publish](previewing.md).

## Remove something

1. Open the item.
2. Choose the ⋯ menu next to **Publish**, then **Delete**.

It comes off the site with the next update. This can't be undone. The status at the top right then reads "Removed from the site" with the time; see [When your change is live](going-live.md). (Removing a comic is different, because of its chapters: see [Publishing comic pages and chapters](publishing-comics.md).)

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Publish** is greyed out, with how many things are left beside it, and a box at the top of the item listing them, such as "Add these before publishing: a title; the picture; at least one portfolio section" | Something required is missing. Each field also shows its own message. | Fill in what's listed. Your work is saved as a draft meanwhile. |
| "Add alt text so people using screen readers can follow it" on a piece | The picture has no alt text. | Add it if you can; you can still publish. |
| "Nothing here yet. Choose the + button above to add one." | Nothing has been added to that section yet. | Choose the + button at the top of the list. |
