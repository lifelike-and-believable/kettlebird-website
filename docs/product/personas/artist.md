## Goals

- Publish new art, comic pages, stories, and products as soon as they are ready, without asking a developer.
- Open or close commissions, and update prices and the about page, when circumstances change.
- Present their work in a way that feels like their own style.

## Skills and tools

- Writer and illustrator; works in drawing software and posts to Tumblr, Bluesky, Instagram, Ko-fi, and AO3.
- Not a developer. Can use a web-based editor; should not need Git, a terminal, or code.

## Context of use

Updates the site around their art schedule: new pieces weekly, commission status and queue as it changes, larger comic or story releases occasionally (docs/CONTENT_STRATEGY.md). Often uploads straight after finishing a piece.

## Frustrations

- Changes that need a developer: the commission banner, about text, and social links today.
- Not knowing when, or whether, a change has reached the live site.
- Publishing work that then looks wrong (cropping, missing alt text) with no preview.
