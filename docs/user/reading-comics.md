## Start a comic

1. Open **Comics** from the menu at the top of any page.
2. Choose a comic. Its page shows the cover, whether it's **Ongoing**, **Complete**, or **On Hiatus**, whether it's an **Original** or a **Fancomic**, and what it's about.
3. Choose **Start reading** to begin at the first page. If the comic has chapters, you can instead choose one from the **Chapters** list to start there. Comics without chapters don't have a list; **Start reading** is all you need.

To catch up on an **Ongoing** comic, choose **Read the newest page** beside **Start reading**. It opens the newest page Star has posted. If an update was several pages long, use **Previous**, or **All pages** to see the chapter's pages and pick up where you left off. Other comics don't have **Read the newest page**: choose **Start reading**, then **Newest**.

If a comic says **Pages coming soon**, Star hasn't posted any pages yet. Check back later.

## Turn pages

Under the page are four buttons, in this order: **First**, **Previous**, **Next** and **Newest**. Each has arrowheads above a word:

- **Previous** and **Next** turn back or forward one page.
- **First** opens the very first page of the comic.
- **Newest** opens the newest page Star has posted, so you can catch up in one tap. On a finished comic it's called **Last** instead, because no new pages are coming.

Arrived from a link partway through? **First** takes you to the start of the comic, and **All pages** shows where you are in this chapter.

With a mouse or keyboard, pointing at a button or moving to it with Tab shows its full name above it, for example "First page of the series". **First** and **Newest** always mean the whole comic, not the chapter you're in.

You can also turn pages in other ways:

- On a phone or tablet, swipe left for the next page and right for the previous one. Nothing on the screen shows this; it just works.
- With a keyboard, press the right and left arrow keys, or use Tab to reach the buttons and press Enter. Tab skips buttons you can't use. Home and End scroll as usual; to jump to the first or newest page, Tab to **First** or **Newest**.
- On a wide screen, pointing with a mouse also shows arrows at the left and right edges of the page.

The line above the page always tells you where you are, for example "Chapter 2: The Long Road · Page 4 of 24". At the end of a chapter, **Next** takes you straight into the next one, and **Previous** on a chapter's first page takes you back to the end of the chapter before.

A button you can't use right now stays where it is, faded with a dashed edge. On the first page of the comic, that's **First** and **Previous**; on the newest page, **Next** and **Newest** (**Last** on a finished comic). A comic with only one page has no buttons at all.

Your browser's back button takes you back through the pages you've read. **Back to series** returns to the comic's page and its chapter list.

If you've zoomed in to look at a panel, swipes and arrow keys move around the page instead of turning it. Zoom back out, or use **Next**.

## Reading on a wide screen

On a laptop, or a tablet held sideways, some comics show two pages side by side, like an open comic: Star chooses, comic by comic. The bar at the top says which, for example "Chapter 2: The Long Road · Pages 2–3 of 12". **Next**, the right arrow key and swiping left turn to the next pages; **Previous**, the left arrow key and swiping right turn back. On a phone, or with your text made much larger, you see one page at a time, as before.

- The first page of a chapter often shows alone on the right, like the first page of a printed comic, and a chapter's last page may show alone on its side.
- A two-page spread is one picture Star drew across two facing pages. On a wide screen it covers both sides, and the bar says "Page 5 of 12 · two-page spread". On a comic that shows one page at a time, a spread still shows wide, on its own.
- On a phone, a spread is shrunk to fit, with "Turn your phone sideways to see it bigger." under it. Held sideways, the spread fills the width, and the buttons are a short scroll below it.
- In **All pages**, a spread's picture is twice as wide (on a phone, it takes two of the three places in a row), marked "5 · spread".
- Every page keeps its own address, so links work on any screen. On those comics, a link to page 3 opens pages 2 and 3 on a laptop, and page 3 on a phone.
- On comics where Star has turned it on, turning shows the page turning over, in under half a second, in most up-to-date browsers. If your device is set to reduce motion, or a page isn't ready yet, the page changes straight away instead.
- With a screen reader, you hear both pages, for example "Chapter 2: The Long Road, pages 2 and 3 of 12.", then what each page shows, left before right. A spread is announced as, for example, "Page 5 of 12, two-page spread.", then what it shows. The buttons' full names say "Next pages" when they lead to two pages, and "Next page" otherwise.

## Read panel by panel

You can read a page panel by panel, each panel enlarged to fit your screen, so small lettering is easy to read without pinching.

1. Choose **Panel by panel** in the bar at the top, beside **All pages**. It's there in chapters whose pages have panels. When it's on, the button is dark.
2. The page zooms into its first panel. The bar says where you are, for example "Page 4 of 12 · panel 1 of 5".
3. **Next**, swiping left or the right arrow key moves to the next panel. After a page's last panel, the next page opens at its first panel. **Previous**, swiping right or the left arrow key goes back, and from a page's first panel to the last panel of the page before.
4. A page without panels, like a full-page picture, shows whole, and the bar says "· whole page". One turn moves past it.
5. To go back to whole pages, choose **Panel by panel** again. You stay on the same page.

- Moving from panel to panel slides and zooms, in under half a second; if your device is set to reduce motion, the view changes straight away.
- **First**, **Newest**, **All pages** and links open a page at its first panel.
- Held sideways, a wide panel fills more of the screen, and the buttons are a short scroll below it; swipes and arrow keys still work.
- Your choice is remembered in that browser.
- When the reader shows two pages side by side, choosing **Panel by panel** shows one page at a time, starting with the page you're on. Choosing it again goes back to two pages side by side.
- With a screen reader, you hear each page's description as it opens, then which panel you're on, for example "Panel 3 of 5." at each step. Switching it on says "Panel by panel." and the panel; switching it off says "Whole page".

## See all the pages in a chapter

Choose **All pages** at the top of the reader to see every page of the chapter you're in as a small picture, numbered and in order. It's handy for finding a scene again, or for seeing how far a chapter goes.

- The page you came from is marked **You were here**, and the sheet opens scrolled to it.
- Choose any picture to open that page.
- **Back to page 4** (with the number of your page) takes you back where you were, and so does your browser's back button. **Back to series** returns to the comic's page. Both are at the top and the bottom of the sheet, so you don't have to scroll back up.

To look at another chapter's pages:

- Choose the line under the heading, for example "Chapter 2 of 6 · All chapters", to unfold the list of chapters, then choose one. Your chapter is marked "you're here".
- Or use **Chapter 1** and **Chapter 3** under the pictures for the chapters either side.

Choosing a chapter here opens its pages as pictures; to start reading it, choose its first picture. Another chapter's sheet marks no page and has no **Back to page** link: to return to the page you were reading, press your browser's back button until you're there.

A chapter with only one page has no **All pages** button. If a comic has no chapters, or only one chapter with pages, the sheet has no chapter list and no chapter links.

## When you reach the end

On the newest page, **Next** and **Newest** (**Last** on a finished comic) fade out, and under the buttons you'll see **You're all caught up!**, a line that tells you what comes next, and **Back to series**:

- **New pages are on the way.** The comic is ongoing; check back for more.
- **This comic is on a break.** The comic is on hiatus and will continue later.
- **That's the end of the story.** The comic is complete.

**Previous** and **First** are still there if you want to look back.

## Share a page

Every page has its own address, and the address in your browser changes as you turn pages. So a link always opens the page you were on, in the reader.

To share the page you're on, choose **Share this page** under the buttons:

- On a phone or tablet, your share menu opens, so you can send the page straight to an app or a friend. If your browser has no share menu, the link is copied instead, as on a computer.
- On a computer, the link is copied, and the button says **Link copied**. Paste it wherever you like.
- If your browser doesn't let the site copy, the link appears in a box, already selected, under **Copy this link:**. Copy it the usual way.

When you post a page's link, the preview shows the comic's cover (or the Kettlebird logo, if it has no cover) and title and where the page is, for example "Kettle Tales · Chapter 2, page 4". It never shows the page itself, so nobody sees it before they get there.

## Using a screen reader or a keyboard

- Every button has a name your screen reader reads, and you can reach each one with Tab. Tab skips buttons you can't use.
- When a new page opens, your screen reader says where you are and what the page shows, for example "Chapter 2: The Long Road, page 4 of 12. A fox looks up at a kettle on a high shelf." If Star hasn't described a page, it says where you are.
- After **Next** or **Previous** (or **First** or **Newest**), the focus stays on that button, so you can keep pressing Enter to turn pages. Arrow keys and swipes also announce the new page, but don't move the focus. Refreshing a page doesn't announce it.
- On the newest page, **Next** can't be used any more, but the focus stays on it, and you'll hear "You're all caught up!" and what comes next. Press Tab to reach **Back to series**, then **Share this page**.
- When you open a page from a link, some screen readers also start reading the page from the top, so you may hear where you are twice.

## If something doesn't look right

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Loading page… If it doesn't appear, try refreshing.** | The page image is still downloading, or didn't arrive. | Wait a moment on a slow connection, or refresh the page. |
| **Page not found** | There's nothing at that address. It may be an old or mistyped link, or Star may have moved or removed the page. | Choose the link with the comic's name, for example **Back to Kettle Tales**, to go to the comic. If there isn't one (the comic is gone, or your browser blocks scripts), choose **Comics** and find it from there. |
| No **Share this page** button | Your browser is blocking the site's scripts. | Copy the address from your browser's address bar instead. |
