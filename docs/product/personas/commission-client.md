## Goals

- Decide whether Star's style suits what they want, from examples of similar work.
- Know the price range, turnaround, and what Star will and will not draw before asking.
- Start a request without back-and-forth about what information to send.

## Skills and tools

- Often a fan already; sometimes an individual buying a gift or a character portrait.
- Used to commissioning through email, social direct messages, or forms.

## Context of use

Usually has a specific idea and sometimes a deadline (a birthday, an event). Compares several artists. Will commit money upfront (a deposit), so trust matters.

## Frustrations

- Not knowing whether commissions are open, or finding out only after writing.
- Unclear terms: revisions, usage rights, refunds. The terms of service say "coming soon" today.
- Writing a long email and then being asked for details they could have given at the start.
