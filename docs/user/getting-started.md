# Getting started with your site's editor

Everything on your site (comics, art, writing, the shop, commissions) is changed in one place: the editor, at **kettlebird.com/studio**. You need nothing else installed, and you never need to visit Sanity, the service the editor runs on, except once to accept your invitation.

Until the site moves to kettlebird.com, the editor is at **kettlebird-website.vercel.app/studio** instead. Everything below works the same there. After the move, that address takes you to the new one, and you sign in once more, the same way.

## Accept your invitation

You'll be told when to expect it: one email from Sanity, inviting you to the **Kettlebird Creations** project. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder.

1. Open the email and choose **Accept invitation**.
2. Sanity asks how you want to sign in. Choose **Google** to use your Google account, or **E-mail / password** to use an email address and a new password. Either way, use the email address the invitation was sent to; with **Google**, check that the account Google shows is that address. If you're also offered GitHub, don't choose it: the editor doesn't offer it.
3. Remember which you chose. That's how you sign in from now on.
4. You end up on Sanity's own project page. You don't need anything there: close it and go to the editor (next section).

If the invitation has expired or you can't find it, ask the person who invited you to send it again.

## Sign in

1. Go to **kettlebird.com/studio**.
2. The sign-in screen says **Kettlebird Creations**. Choose the same option as when you accepted, **Google** or **E-mail / password**, with the same email address.
3. The editor opens: **Kettlebird Creations** at the top left, and the menu of things you can edit on the left. Bookmark this page now, so you can come straight back.

The circle with your initial or photo at the top right is your account. Choose it to see your name, your email address, and **Sign out**.

On the same computer and browser, you usually stay signed in, and the editor opens straight away. If you see the sign-in screen again, sign in the same way. On a new computer, or another browser, you sign in once, the same way.

## Using the editor

The editor works in any up-to-date browser. It works on a phone, but it's cramped there; use a computer for uploading comic pages.

The menu on the left lists everything you can edit: **Comics**, **Art**, **Writing**, **Shop**, **Commissions** and **Site settings**. If it shows older names instead, the tidier menu hasn't arrived yet. Until it does: "Comic Series" and "Chapter" are your comics, **Gallery Item** is your art, "Written Work" is your writing, "Product" is the shop, and "Commission Type" is your commissions.

Visitors to your site never see a link to the editor. Only people who have been invited can see or change anything in it.

Next: [Publishing comic pages and chapters](publishing-comics.md), and [Editing your art, writing, shop and commissions](editing-your-site.md).

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A screen saying you don't have access, with **Sign out** | You signed in a different way from when you accepted the invitation, or with a different Google account (easy to do if your browser is signed in to two). The screen shows which address you used. | Choose **Sign out**, then sign in the same way you accepted, with the email address the invitation went to. |
| You can't remember whether you chose Google or a password | | Try **Google** with the invited address. If that says you don't have access, choose **Sign out** and try **E-mail / password**. |
| You've forgotten your password | | Under **E-mail / password**, choose "Forgot password?". Sanity emails you a link to set a new one. |
| The sign-in screen again, when you were signed in before | Your browser forgot you: you cleared its history, used a private window, or it's been a while. | Sign in again, the same way. Nothing you saved is lost. |
| "No access" at kettlebird.sanity.studio | That's an old editor address, no longer used. | Delete that bookmark and use **kettlebird.com/studio**. |
| A screen saying this address isn't allowed to sign in | You opened a test copy of the site, not the real one. | Use **kettlebird.com/studio**. |
| The invitation expired, or you can't find the email | | Ask the person who invited you to send it again. |
