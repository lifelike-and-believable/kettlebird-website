## Goals

- See more of Star's art after finding one piece on Tumblr, Bluesky, or Instagram.
- Read Star's comics and stories, and pick up where they left off when a new page or chapter appears.
- Support Star by buying a print, sticker, or digital item, or by tipping.

## Skills and tools

- Arrives from a social post or shared link, usually on a phone.
- Comfortable with fandom sites (Tumblr, AO3) and webcomic readers; expects swiping and tapping to work.
- May use a screen reader, keyboard, or zoom.

## Context of use

Browses in short sessions, often from a social feed, and decides within seconds whether to keep looking. Comes back when Star posts something new.

## Frustrations

- Links that go nowhere, such as the comic series links today.
- Having to leave the site to read or buy, and losing their place when they do.
- Slow image-heavy pages on a phone connection.
