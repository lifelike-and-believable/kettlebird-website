# See it before you publish

<!-- docs/user/previewing.md. For Star (persona-artist): previewing an art piece, a chapter, a
series or Site settings before publishing. Written from design-preview. publishing-comics.md,
editing-your-site.md and site-settings.md point here. -->

While you edit something, you can see it on its site page beside the form, exactly as fans will see it once you publish: the crop, the line breaks, how a long title wraps, on a computer and on a phone. Nobody else sees it, and nothing goes on the site until you choose **Publish**.

## Open the preview

**Preview** arrives with a later update to the editor; until then, the button isn't there, and this guide is for when it is.

1. Open the item you're working on: an art piece, a chapter, a series, or one of the four items in **Site settings**. Stories, products and commission types can be previewed too.
2. Choose **Preview**, beside **Publish** at the bottom of the item.
3. The menu and list fold away. Your form stays on the left, and the site page appears on the right.

Above the page you'll see:

- which page it is, and when the preview last caught up, such as "Updated just now";
- **Computer** and **Phone**, to switch widths;
- for an art piece, **Show it opened**;
- a line that stays put while you scroll: "Preview: not on the site yet. This item's unpublished changes are shown, outlined; everything else is as published."

To close it, choose **Hide preview**. The menu and list come back.

## What the preview shows

- Your changes, outlined. The parts you've changed have a dashed outline, so you can find them at a glance. The outline isn't on the real site.
- Only this item's changes. Everything else on the page is as it is on the site now. If you're halfway through your bio in another tab, it doesn't show up while you preview a comic page. The preview shows exactly what **Publish** on this item will do.
- Changes the page can't show. Some changes don't appear on the page itself, like alt text, or something you removed. These are listed in a yellow line under the top line, for example "Not visible on the page: alt text", "Removed: Instagram link" or "Removed from the page: How it works". Check them in the form.

As you type, change a picture or move a crop, the preview catches up, usually a second or two after you pause. "Updated just now" refreshes when it has.

## Computer and phone

- **Computer** shows the page as it looks on a laptop, shrunk to fit beside the form, so you see the computer layout even if your window is narrow.
- **Phone** shows the page at a phone's width, full size. Most fans read on phones, so it's worth a look before you publish a comic page.

The editor remembers which one you chose last.

Links in the preview don't go anywhere. Pointing at one says "Links don't work in the preview".

## Which page you see

| What you're editing | What the preview shows |
|---|---|
| An art piece | Your portfolio, scrolled to the piece in its first portfolio section. If it's in other sections too, the yellow line names them. **Show it opened** opens the piece the way fans see it when they choose it. |
| A chapter | The reader, at the page open in the chapter's page panel, or page 1 if none is open. To check another page, open it in the chapter. If you move the open page, the preview follows it. |
| A series | The series' page, from the top. |
| **Site settings**: **Commissions** | The Commissions page, from the top. |
| **Site settings**: **About page** | The about page, scrolled to what you changed. |
| **Site settings**: **Home page** | The home page, scrolled to your artist text. |
| **Site settings**: **Contact and social links** | The home page, scrolled to your social links and the footer. |
| A story, product or commission type | The writing page, the shop or the commissions page, scrolled to the item. |

## After you publish

When you choose **Publish** with the preview open, the top line turns green: "Published. The site matches this preview once the status at the top right says Live." When the status says **Live**, the outlines and the yellow line go away, because nothing is left unpublished. [When your change is live](going-live.md) explains the status.

The preview never changes what's on the site, and **View on site** always shows what fans see, never your drafts.

## If the preview can't show something

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Choose a portfolio section to see where this piece goes." | An art piece isn't in a portfolio section yet. | Tick one under **Portfolio sections**. |
| "Add the picture to preview it." | An art piece has no picture yet. | Add the picture. |
| "Add a web address so readers can find this comic. The preview needs it too." | The series (or the chapter's series) has no web address: the same problem the series form points out. | Fill in **Web address** on the series. |
| "Add a page to preview this chapter." | The chapter has no pages yet. Fans never see a chapter without pages. | Add a page. |
| "Can't show the preview right now." with **Try again**, and "Your changes are saved. You can still publish." | Your connection dropped, or the site didn't answer. | Choose **Try again**. |
| The page looks dimmed | The preview is catching up with your latest change. | Wait a moment; the old preview stays until the new one is ready. |
| A new picture isn't in the preview yet | It's still uploading. | Wait for the upload to finish. |

Something you've never published, like a new series, shows in the preview as if it were already on the site, outlined.

If two chapters have the same number, the preview still shows the chapter, and the yellow line says "Chapter 2 already exists in this series; publishing is blocked until you change one". (The form says "Chapter 2 already exists in this series. Choose another number.") Change the number on one of them; you can't publish until you do.
