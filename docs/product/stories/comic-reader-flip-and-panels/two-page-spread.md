# Two-page spread

<!-- docs/product/stories/comic-reader-flip-and-panels/two-page-spread.md. Story 1 of epic-comic-reader-flip-and-panels. -->

As a fan on a wide screen, I want to see two pages side by side, like an open comic, so that spreads and facing pages read the way Star drew them.

## Acceptance criteria

- On a screen wide enough for two pages, the reader shows two facing pages at once; on a narrower screen it shows one page at a time, as now.
- Each chapter pairs its pages as Star chose for it: page 1 alone on the right, as in a printed book, then 2 and 3, 4 and 5; or 1 and 2, 3 and 4 from the start.
- Star can mark a single page image as a two-page spread. On a wide screen it covers both sides of the open comic; on a narrow screen it is shrunk to fit the screen, like every page. It is never split across a page turn, and the pairing continues after it.
- A chapter's last page, when it has no facing page, shows on its own side.
- Every page keeps its own address, and a shared link to a page opens the spread it is in.
- Turning a page by keyboard, tap, swipe or click moves by a whole spread on a wide screen and by one page on a narrow one; the contact sheet, play controls and screen reader announcements keep working.
