# Help where I'm working

As Star, I want the guide for what I'm editing one click away in the editor, so that I can check how something works without asking anyone or knowing where the guides are kept.

## Acceptance criteria

- Star's guides (getting started, editing the site, publishing comics, Site settings, previewing, and going live) can be read on the site at a help address, in the site's own style, and comfortably on a phone.
- The help pages aren't in the site's menu, aren't offered to search engines, and don't change anything fans see.
- Links between guides, and to a section of a guide, take Star to that guide or section on the site.
- When a guide changes and the change is merged, the help page shows the new version once the site updates, with no other step.
- The editor has a **Help** entry, always in the same place, that lists the guides by their titles and opens the one Star chooses.
- Each kind of item Star edits (art piece, chapter, series, story, product, commission type, and each Site settings item) has a link at the top that opens the guide section for that kind of item.
- Help opens in a new tab, so Star's place in the editor and anything unpublished stay where they were.
- The links work by keyboard and with a screen reader, and their names say where they go.
