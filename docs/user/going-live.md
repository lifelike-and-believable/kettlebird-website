# When your change is live

<!-- docs/user/going-live.md. For Star (persona-artist): what happens after Publish, and how the
editor says when fans can see a change. Written from design-know-when-live. The other guides
(publishing-comics.md, editing-your-site.md) point here. -->

Publishing is the only step. When you choose **Publish**, or delete something, the site updates on its own, usually within a minute or two. The status at the top right of the editor, next to your account circle, always says where your latest change is.

## After you publish

1. A short note appears near **Publish**: "Published. Updating the site; the status at the top right says when it's live."
2. The status reads **Updating the site…**. You can keep working, publish more, or close the editor; the update carries on without you.
3. When fans can see your change, it reads **Live** with the time the site finished updating, like "Live · 2:41 pm".
4. Choose **View on site** to open, in a new tab, the page your latest change appears on (below). If you already have the site open in another tab, reload it to see the change.

When you open the editor, the status shows your last published change, for example **Live** · Yesterday, 2:41 pm. Saving a draft doesn't change it. The status is worked out from the site and the editor together, so it's the same in every tab, after you close the editor, and on another computer.

Times are in your own time zone: "2:41 pm" today, "Yesterday, 2:41 pm", or a date for older changes.

## Where View on site goes

It opens the page of your latest change:

- a chapter: its first page in the reader;
- a comic series: the series' page;
- a piece of art: your portfolio;
- your about text: the about page;
- commissions and their prices: the commissions page;
- a product: the shop;
- the home page's text, or your social links: the home page.

After several changes, it opens the page of the last one; the others are live too.

## Publishing several things

You never need to wait between changes. One **Publish** is one change: a whole chapter with all its pages counts as one. If you publish more before the site has caught up, the status counts them, "Updating the site… (3 changes)", and turns **Live** once the site shows all of them. The 5- and 15-minute times below count from your latest change.

## What the status can say

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Updating the site…** | Your latest change is on its way to the site. | Nothing. |
| **Live** · 2:41 pm, with **View on site** | Fans can see your latest change, since that time. | Choose **View on site** to check it. |
| **Removed from the site** · 2:41 pm | Something you deleted is gone from the site, since that time. There's no **View on site**: there's nothing to view. | Nothing. |
| **Still updating, taking longer than usual…** | The update has taken more than 5 minutes. | Nothing; it usually finishes on its own. |
| **Didn't go live** | After 15 minutes, your change still isn't on the site. | Choose **Didn't go live** to read more ("Your change is saved, but the site didn't update."); **Close** or Escape closes it. The person who looks after the site is told automatically and will fix it. You can keep working and publishing. When it's fixed, the status turns **Live** on its own: you don't need to publish again. Wait until it says **Live** before telling people your change is up. |
| **Can't check the site right now** | Your connection dropped, or the site couldn't be reached. | Nothing; the editor tries again every 15 seconds and never says **Live** without seeing it. |

Saved changes you haven't published are drafts. The item shows the editor's "not published" mark, the status ignores them, and fans see the last published version until you publish.
