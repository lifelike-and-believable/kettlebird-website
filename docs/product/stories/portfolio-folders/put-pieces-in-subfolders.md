# Put pieces in subfolders

<!-- docs/product/stories/portfolio-folders/put-pieces-in-subfolders.md. Story 3 of epic-portfolio-folders. -->

As Star, I want to put a piece in a subfolder of a folder it's in, in the editor, so that the Portfolio groups my work the way I think of it.

## Acceptance criteria

- A piece in **Art** has a field for its subfolder, beside **Portfolio sections**. Star types a subfolder's name, or picks one already used, so the same fandom or client isn't spelled two ways. It's optional: a piece without one shows loose in its folder.
- A subfolder exists while at least one published piece is in it; there's nothing else to create or delete. Renaming a subfolder means changing its name on its pieces; the editor lists the names in use, with how many pieces each has.
- The field's help text says what a subfolder is and where it shows, in plain words, as the other fields do.
- The piece's row in the **Art** list shows its subfolder after its sections.
- Star's guide `editing-your-site.md` says how to put pieces in a subfolder, rename one, and take a piece out; it's an existing guide, so the help menu needs nothing new.
- Publishing a piece with a subfolder puts it there on the site with the next update, as any change does (design-know-when-live).
