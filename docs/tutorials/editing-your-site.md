# Tutorial: find your way round the editor

You'll look round the menu, add a piece of art, see what happens when something required is missing, add a commission type, and remove what you added.

## Steps

1. Open the editor and sign in. The menu on the left lists **Comics**, **Art**, **Writing**, **Shop**, **Commissions** and **Site settings**, and nothing else.
2. Choose **Site settings**. You see "Site settings is coming in the next update."
3. Choose **Art**, then the + button, **New piece**. Without filling anything in, look at the bottom: "3 things to add before publishing", and **Publish** can't be pressed.
4. Type `Tutorial piece` into **Title (required)**. The badge says "2 things to add before publishing".
5. Upload your picture into **Picture (required)**, and choose Original Work under **Portfolio sections (choose at least one)**. The badge goes, and **Publish** can be pressed.
6. Leave **Alt text** empty. It says "Add alt text so people using screen readers can follow it", but **Publish** still works. Choose **Publish**.
7. Go back to **Art**. "Tutorial piece" is in the list, with "Original Work" under it.
8. Choose **Commissions**, then the + button, **New commission type**. Type `Tutorial sketch` into **Name (required)**, `$20–40` into **Price range**, and `99` into **Order on the page**. Choose **Publish**. In the list, "Tutorial sketch" is last, with "$20–40" under it.
9. Choose **Shop**, then the + button. **Shop section (required)** offers Prints, Stickers and Digital. Close it without publishing.
10. Open "Tutorial piece", choose the ⋯ menu next to **Publish**, then **Delete**. Do the same for "Tutorial sketch".

## What you found

Each section lists one kind of thing, required fields say so before you miss them, the editor tells you what's missing before you can publish, and every field says what it does on the site. Write down anything that was hard to find, or any help text that was unclear.
