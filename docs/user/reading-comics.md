## Start a comic

1. Open **Comics** from the menu at the top of any page.
2. Choose a comic. Its page shows the cover, whether it's **Ongoing**, **Complete**, or **On Hiatus**, whether it's an **Original** or a **Fancomic**, and what it's about.
3. Choose **Start reading** to begin at the first page. If the comic has chapters, you can instead choose one from the **Chapters** list to start there. Comics without chapters don't have a list; **Start reading** is all you need.

If a comic says **Pages coming soon**, Star hasn't posted any pages yet. Check back later.

## Turn pages

- Tap or click **Next page** and **Previous page** under the page.
- On a phone or tablet, you can also swipe left for the next page and right for the previous one. Nothing on the screen shows this; it just works.
- With a keyboard, press the right and left arrow keys, or use Tab to reach **Next page** and **Previous page** and press Enter.

The line above the page always tells you where you are, for example "Chapter 2: The Long Road · Page 4 of 24". At the end of a chapter, **Next page** takes you straight into the next one, and **Previous page** on a chapter's first page takes you back to the end of the chapter before. The first page of a comic has no **Previous page**.

Your browser's back button takes you back through the pages you've read. **Back to series** returns to the comic's page and its chapter list.

If you've zoomed in to look at a panel, swipes and arrow keys move around the page instead of turning it. Zoom back out, or use **Next page**.

## When you reach the end

On the last page, **Next page** is replaced by **You're all caught up!** and a line that tells you what comes next:

- **New pages are on the way.** The comic is ongoing; check back for more.
- **This comic is on a break.** The comic is on hiatus and will continue later.
- **That's the end of the story.** The comic is complete.

**Previous page** is still there if you want to look back.

## If something doesn't look right

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Loading page… If it doesn't appear, try refreshing.** | The page image is still downloading, or didn't arrive. | Wait a moment on a slow connection, or refresh the page. |
| **Page not found** | There's nothing at that address. It may be an old or mistyped link. | Choose **Comics** and find the comic from there. |

Using a screen reader? Notes on how pages are announced will be added here with the reader's accessibility work.
