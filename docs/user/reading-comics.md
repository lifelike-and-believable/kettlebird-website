## Start a comic

1. Open **Comics** from the menu at the top of any page.
2. Choose a comic. Its page shows the cover, whether it's **Ongoing**, **Complete**, or **On Hiatus**, whether it's an **Original** or a **Fancomic**, and what it's about.
3. Choose **Start reading** to begin at the first page. If the comic has chapters, you can instead choose one from the **Chapters** list to start there. Comics without chapters don't have a list; **Start reading** is all you need.

If a comic says **Pages coming soon**, Star hasn't posted any pages yet. Check back later.

## Turn pages

Under the page are four buttons, in this order: **First**, **Previous**, **Next** and **Newest**. Each has arrowheads above a word:

- **Previous** and **Next** turn back or forward one page.
- **First** opens the very first page of the comic.
- **Newest** opens the newest page Star has posted, so you can catch up in one tap. On a finished comic it's called **Last** instead, because no new pages are coming.

Arrived from a link partway through? **First** takes you to the start of the comic, and **All pages** shows where you are in this chapter.

With a mouse or keyboard, pointing at a button or moving to it with Tab shows its full name above it, for example "First page of the series". **First** and **Newest** always mean the whole comic, not the chapter you're in.

You can also turn pages in other ways:

- On a phone or tablet, swipe left for the next page and right for the previous one. Nothing on the screen shows this; it just works.
- With a keyboard, press the right and left arrow keys, or use Tab to reach the buttons and press Enter. Tab skips buttons you can't use. Home and End scroll as usual; to jump to the first or newest page, Tab to **First** or **Newest**.
- On a wide screen, pointing with a mouse also shows arrows at the left and right edges of the page.

The line above the page always tells you where you are, for example "Chapter 2: The Long Road · Page 4 of 24". At the end of a chapter, **Next** takes you straight into the next one, and **Previous** on a chapter's first page takes you back to the end of the chapter before.

A button you can't use right now stays where it is, faded with a dashed edge. On the first page of the comic, that's **First** and **Previous**; on the newest page, **Next** and **Newest** (**Last** on a finished comic). A comic with only one page has no buttons at all.

Your browser's back button takes you back through the pages you've read. **Back to series** returns to the comic's page and its chapter list.

If you've zoomed in to look at a panel, swipes and arrow keys move around the page instead of turning it. Zoom back out, or use **Next**.

## See all the pages in a chapter

Choose **All pages** at the top of the reader to see every page of the chapter you're in as a small picture, numbered and in order. It's handy for finding a scene again, or for seeing how far a chapter goes.

- The page you came from is marked **You were here**, and the sheet opens scrolled to it.
- Choose any picture to open that page.
- **← Back to page 4** (with the number of your page) takes you back where you were, and so does your browser's back button. **Back to series** returns to the comic's page. Both are at the top and the bottom of the sheet, so you don't have to scroll back up.

To look at another chapter's pages:

- Choose the line under the heading, for example "Chapter 2 of 6 · All chapters", to unfold the list of chapters, then choose one. Your chapter is marked "you're here".
- Or use **← Chapter 1** and **Chapter 3 →** under the pictures for the chapters either side.

Choosing a chapter here opens its pages as pictures; to start reading it, choose its first picture. Another chapter's sheet marks no page and has no **← Back to page** link: to return to the page you were reading, press your browser's back button until you're there.

A chapter with only one page has no **All pages** button. If a comic has no chapters, or only one chapter with pages, the sheet has no chapter list and no chapter links.

## When you reach the end

On the newest page, **Next** and **Newest** (**Last** on a finished comic) fade out, and under the buttons you'll see **You're all caught up!**, a line that tells you what comes next, and **Back to series**:

- **New pages are on the way.** The comic is ongoing; check back for more.
- **This comic is on a break.** The comic is on hiatus and will continue later.
- **That's the end of the story.** The comic is complete.

**Previous** and **First** are still there if you want to look back.

## If something doesn't look right

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Loading page… If it doesn't appear, try refreshing.** | The page image is still downloading, or didn't arrive. | Wait a moment on a slow connection, or refresh the page. |
| **Page not found** | There's nothing at that address. It may be an old or mistyped link. | Choose **Comics** and find the comic from there. |

Using a screen reader? Notes on how pages are announced will be added here with the reader's accessibility work.
