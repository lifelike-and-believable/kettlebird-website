# Commissions, your about page, the home page and your links

Everything here is in **Site settings**, the last section of the editor's menu. It has four items:

- **Commissions**: whether commissions are open or closed.
- **About page**: your portrait, bio, artist statement and quick facts.
- **Home page**: the short artist text on the home page.
- **Contact and social links**: the email address people write to, and your social links.

Each item is published on its own. Choose one, change it, and choose **Publish**: only that item goes live. A half-finished bio never goes out with a commission change, and a problem in one item never stops you publishing another. You can't delete these four items, or make copies of them.

After **Publish**, the site updates on its own, and the status at the top right says when your change is live ([When your change is live](going-live.md)). **View on site** opens the page the item changes: the Commissions page, the about page, or the home page (for the home page text, and for your contact email and social links).

## Before you start: check your quick facts

The **About page** item starts with what your about page shows today, including four quick facts: pronouns, where you're based, what you make, and what you love. Choose **Site settings**, then **About page**, and check them first. Change anything that isn't right, and choose **Publish**.

## Open or close commissions

To close commissions:

1. **Site settings**, then **Commissions**.
2. Under **Commissions are…**, choose **Closed**.
3. If you like, write a **Note when closed**, like "Back in March." It's shown after "Commissions are closed." exactly as you write it, so end it with a full stop, as in that example. Up to 120 characters. Leave it empty for no note.
4. **Publish**.

To open them again: **Site settings**, then **Commissions**, choose **Open**, then **Publish**. The note is only shown while commissions are closed, so you can leave it there, but check it the next time you close them: it comes back as you left it.

What visitors see:

| | Open | Closed |
|---|---|---|
| The banner at the top of the Commissions page | Green: "Commissions are open. Email me to get started." with **Contact me** | Orange: "Commissions are closed." and your note |
| The rest of the Commissions page | Your commission types and prices, How it works, and Ready to order? with **Email me to get started** | "These are the kinds of commission I take, for when they open again.", then your commission types and prices. How it works, Ready to order? and the email buttons are left out. At the end: "Commissions are closed for now. To hear when they open, follow me on the social links at the bottom of the page." (left out if you have no social links) |
| Get in touch, on the about page | Reads "For commission inquiries, collaboration ideas, or just to say hi:" | Reads "To ask about collaborations, or just to say hi:" |

Your commission types themselves (names, prices, examples) are in the **Commissions** section of the menu, not here: see [Editing your art, writing, shop and commissions](editing-your-site.md).

Don't mention whether commissions are open in your home page text or your bio: the Commissions page says that, and changes when you do.

## Your about page

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Portrait** | A picture of you, or a self-portrait. Shown at the top of the about page, and beside the home page's artist text. To choose what stays in view when it's cropped, choose the picture's **Edit** button. |
| **Portrait's alt text** | What the portrait shows, for people using screen readers. If it's empty, screen readers skip the portrait, and the editor marks it as missing. |
| **Hello! (your bio)** | A few paragraphs about you, under the heading **Hello!** |
| **The Work (your artist statement)** | What your work is about, under the heading **The Work**. |
| **At a glance** | Short facts in the about page's side box, in this order. Each has a **Label** ("Based in") and a **Value** ("Vancouver, BC"). |

To change a fact, choose it: a box opens with its **Label** and **Value**; choose **Done** when you've finished. To add one, choose "+ Add a fact" under the list. To change the order, drag the dotted grip (⠿) at the left of a fact's row. Choose **Remove** to take a fact away.

To add or change your portrait:

1. **Site settings**, then **About page**.
2. In **Portrait**, upload your picture, or drag it onto the field.
3. Fill in **Portrait's alt text**.
4. **Publish**.

Anything you leave empty is left off the page entirely, with no empty heading or box. **About the Artist** and **Get in touch** always show.

## The home page

**Artist text** is a sentence or two about you, under the home page's social links, with your portrait (from **About page**) beside it. Leave it empty to leave the block out; the home page then ends after the social links.

## Your contact email and social links

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Contact email** (required) | Where every email button on the site sends people: the footer's Contact, the about page's Get in touch, and the Commissions page's buttons. |
| **Social links** | Shown in this order on the home page, in every page's footer, and on the about page. Each has a **Name**, as it shows on the button ("Instagram"), and a **Link**, the page it opens. |

Each link's row shows its name and its link, like "Instagram · https://www.instagram.com/kettlebirdart". To change a link, choose its row, change its **Name** or **Link**, and choose **Done**. To add one, choose "+ Add a link" under the list. To change the order, drag the dotted grip (⠿) at the left of a link's row. Choose **Remove** to take a link away.

For a **Link**, copy the address from your browser's address bar while you're on that page: it starts with https://. Email addresses go in **Contact email**, not as links.

The Ko-fi button is called "Ko-fi ☕" everywhere once you publish this item. Today the about page calls it "Support on Ko-fi ☕", so that's the one change you'll see the first time you publish, even if you changed nothing else.

The buttons' colours go green, yellow, orange and pink in order, then start again, so every link gets one, however many you have. With no links at all, the home page's row of buttons and the footer's **Connect** heading are left out.

## Writing the text

All the text here is plain: no bold or italics. Leave a blank line between paragraphs to start a new one. A field with only spaces in it counts as empty.

## If something goes wrong

When something needs fixing, **Publish** is greyed out, "1 thing to fix before publishing" (or how many) sits beside it, and a box at the top of the item lists what to fix, like "Fix this before publishing: Instagram: the link isn't a web address". The field itself shows its message too. Only that item is held back: you can still publish the other three.

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This isn't a web address. Copy the link from your browser's address bar; it starts with https:// (or http://)." | Open the page in your browser, copy the whole address from the address bar, and paste it in. It can't have spaces. |
| "Give this link a name, as it shows on the button." | Add the **Name**, like "Bluesky", or **Remove** the link. |
| "Add the page this link opens." | Add the **Link**, or **Remove** the link. |
| "This doesn't look like an email address." | Check the **Contact email** for typos; it needs an @. |
| "Add the email address people should write to." | **Contact email** is required. |
| "Fill in both the label and the value, or remove this fact." | A fact needs both, like "Based in" and "Vancouver, BC". |
| "Keep the note under 120 characters, so it fits the banner on a phone." | Shorten the **Note when closed**. |
| "Add alt text so people using screen readers know what the portrait shows." | Describe the portrait in **Portrait's alt text**. This one doesn't stop you publishing. |
| The site still shows the old text | Check the status at the top right: it may still be **Updating the site…**. Once it reads **Live**, reload the page. See [When your change is live](going-live.md). |
