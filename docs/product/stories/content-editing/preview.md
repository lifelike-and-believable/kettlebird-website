# Preview before publishing

<!-- docs/product/stories/content-editing/preview.md. Story 5 of epic-content-editing. -->

As Star, I want to see a piece, page, or text change as it will look on the site before I publish it, so that I catch crops and mistakes first.

## Acceptance criteria

- While editing an art piece, a comic page, a series, or Site settings, Star can open a preview that shows the site page it appears on, with the unpublished change in place.
- The preview updates as Star types or changes an image, without publishing.
- The preview is clearly marked as a preview, and visitors to the real site never see unpublished changes.
- Star can see the preview at phone width as well as computer width.
