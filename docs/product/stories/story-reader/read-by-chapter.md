# Read by chapter

<!-- docs/product/stories/story-reader/read-by-chapter.md. Story 2 of epic-story-reader. -->

As a fan, I want a story with chapters to show its chapter list and let me move from one chapter to the next, so that I can read a long story the way I would on AO3 and come back to the chapter I was on.

## Acceptance criteria

- A story with chapters lists them, in order, with their titles, on the story's page.
- Each chapter has its own page and address, showing the story's title, the chapter's number and title, and its text.
- From a chapter, a fan can go to the previous and next chapters and back to the chapter list, by keyboard as well as by tapping or clicking.
- A one-shot has no chapter list and no chapter numbers.
- A chapter's address keeps working when chapters are added after it.
