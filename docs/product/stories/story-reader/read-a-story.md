# Read a story

<!-- docs/product/stories/story-reader/read-a-story.md. Story 1 of epic-story-reader. -->

As a fan, I want to open a story from the Writing page and read it on the site in one readable column, with its title, cover and genres, so that I can read Star's writing without leaving for another site.

## Acceptance criteria

- A story with its text on the site has its own page, which the Writing page's card opens.
- The page shows the story's title, cover (if it has one), genres and its text, in one column that's comfortable to read on a phone and on a computer, with no sideways scrolling.
- The text keeps Star's paragraphs, italics, bold, scene breaks and pictures. A picture shows where Star put it, at the column's width or its own, whichever is smaller, with its alt text for screen readers, and can be seen full screen.
- A story Star marks as having mature or explicit content says so on its card and page, and asks before showing its text, once per browser. Any content notes they write show before the text (Star, 2026-11-07).
- A story that's also on AO3 still offers **Read on AO3** on its page and its card.
- A story with no text on the site keeps working as now: its card offers **Read on AO3** and has no page of its own.
- The page works with a screen reader, by keyboard, at 200% text, and with the browser's reader mode.
