# Putting stories on the site

<!-- docs/user/publishing-stories.md. For Star (persona-artist): putting a story, one-shot or in
chapters, on the site with the editor. Written from design-story-reader. -->

Your stories can be read right on the site, in one comfortable column, on a phone or a computer. You put a story up by pasting it in from Google Docs or Word, and a long story can go up a chapter at a time, the way comic chapters do.

**Stories on the site** arrive with a later update to the editor; until then, the Writing page works as it does now, and this guide is for when they're there.

Everything here happens in the editor, at `kettlebird.com/studio`, in **Writing**. **Publish** is at the bottom of the open item, and the status at the top right says when your change is live ([When your change is live](going-live.md)).

## Start a new story

1. In **Writing**, choose **New story**, and type the **Title**. The **Web address** fills itself in from the title: it's the end of the story's link, like `kettlebird.com/writing/the-kettles-song`. Once people have shared links, leave it alone.
2. Choose a **Rating**: General, Teen, Mature, Explicit, or Not rated. It shows before the story and on the Writing page.
3. If readers should know something before they start, write it in **Content notes**, such as "Mild violence". It shows under the rating.
4. Add a **Short description**, a **Cover** and **Genres** if you like, as before.
5. Then either paste the whole story into **Text** (a one-shot), or add chapters (below).
6. Choose **Publish**.

A story rated Mature or Explicit asks readers before showing its text: they see your rating and notes, and **Continue to the story**. General, Teen and Not rated stories open straight to the text.

## Paste your text

Copy the story (or the chapter) from Google Docs or Word, click in **Text**, and paste.

- Paragraphs, *italics* and **bold** come through as you wrote them.
- Headings, lists, tables and links come through as plain paragraphs, and pictures are left out. The text box only offers **Italic**, **Bold** and **Scene break**, so what you see is what readers get.
- Extra empty lines between paragraphs are left out on the site.

To fix a word after pasting, just type in the box, as in a document.

## Scene breaks

A scene break shows on the site as a small centred ⁂, with space around it. In the editor it shows as a "Scene break" bar. You get one by:

- choosing **Scene break** in the text box's toolbar, where your cursor is;
- a line with only symbols on it, like `* * *`, `***`, `~` or `#`, which turns into a scene break when you paste it or move off the line;
- a horizontal line in Word or Google Docs, or a gap of two or more empty lines, which turn into a scene break when you paste.

To remove one, click the bar and press Delete.

## Add a chapter to a story

For a story in chapters:

1. Open the story and choose **Add chapter**, under **Chapters**. The chapter opens beside the story, with the next number filled in.
2. Type a **Title** if the chapter has one, like "Over the Ridge". Without one, it's shown as "Chapter 3".
3. Paste the chapter into **Text**.
4. Choose **Publish**. A new story is published along with its first chapter.

Chapters are listed under the story in number order, each marked **On the site** or **Not published yet**. Readers only see published chapters.

On the story, set **Status** to **In progress** while you're still posting chapters, and **Complete** when the last one is out. Readers see it on the Writing page, and at the end of the newest chapter ("More to come." or "The end.").

If you started a story as a one-shot and want to split it, choose **Add chapter** on it: Chapter 1 opens with your story's text already in it, and the story's own **Text** is empty. Nothing is lost. Cut the text you want into Chapter 2, and so on.

## Ratings and content notes

| Rating | Use it for |
|---|---|
| General | Fine for anyone. |
| Teen | Some violence, swearing or mature themes. |
| Mature | Strong violence, sex or themes, not explicit. Readers are asked before the text shows. |
| Explicit | Explicit content. Readers are asked before the text shows. |
| Not rated | You'd rather not say. With no content notes, nothing shows before the story. |

Content notes show exactly as you write them, under the rating. Keep them short: a list of what to expect works best.

## Check it, then publish

Choose **Preview** at the top of a story or a chapter to see its page before you publish ([See it before you publish](previewing.md)). The preview shows the text whatever the rating.

After **Publish**, **View on site** opens the story's page, or the chapter's.

## Links elsewhere

**Link to read it** is optional. For a story with its text on the site, readers see it as "Also on AO3" (or Ko-fi, Tumblr, Wattpad). While AO3 is offline, you may want to remove AO3 links, so readers don't follow a link that doesn't work.

A story with no text on the site works as before: its **Read it** button on the Writing page opens the link.

## If links will change

Changing the **Web address** of a published story, or the **Chapter** number of a published chapter, breaks links people have shared. The editor warns you before you publish, as it does for comics.

## Remove a story

Choose ⋯ next to **Publish**, then **Delete**. For a story in chapters, the editor asks once, naming the chapters: "Delete The Long Road and its 3 chapters?" The story and its chapters come off the site with the next update, and links people have shared stop working. This can't be undone.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Add a web address so readers can find this story." | A story with text or chapters needs its web address. | Fill in **Web address**, or choose the button beside it to make one from the title. |
| "Choose a rating, or Not rated." | A story with text or chapters needs a rating. | Choose one under **Rating**. |
| "Chapter 2 already exists in this story. Choose another number." | Two chapters have the same number. | Change the number on one of them. |
| "Add the chapter's text." | The chapter is empty. | Paste or type its text. |
| "This story has chapters, so its text is in them." | The story has chapters, so its own **Text** isn't used. | Edit the text in the chapters. |
| Italics are missing after pasting | Some apps copy text without formatting. | Copy from Google Docs or Word, or select the words and choose **Italic**. |
| A scene break shows as `* * *` text in the editor | It was typed in the middle of other text. | Put it on its own line. |
