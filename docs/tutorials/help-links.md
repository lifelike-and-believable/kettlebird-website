# Tutorial: find help from the editor

You'll open a guide from **Help**, open the part of a guide about a chapter, and read a help page on a phone.

## Steps

1. Open `/studio` and sign in. At the top right, just left of the status (such as **Live**), there's **Help**. Further left, near your account circle, is Sanity's own **?**.
2. Choose **Help**. A list opens: "Getting started with your site's editor", "Editing your art, writing, shop and commissions", "Publishing comic pages and chapters", "Commissions, your about page, the home page and your links", "See it before you publish", "When your change is live", then **All help**. Press the down arrow twice, then Escape: the list closes and **Help** has the focus again.
3. Choose **Help**, then "Commissions, your about page, the home page and your links". It opens in a new tab, in the site's look, with **On this page** under the title. Choose "Open or close commissions" in **On this page**: the page moves to that section. Close the tab; the editor is as you left it.
4. In **Comics**, open a series, then a chapter. At the top left of the chapter, on the same line as **Preview**, is "Help: Add a chapter ↗". Choose it: "Publishing comic pages and chapters" opens in a new tab at "Add a chapter".
5. Back in the editor, choose **Preview**. The help line stays on the left and **Hide preview** on the right; a long one wraps. Choose **Hide preview**.
6. In **Site settings**, open **Contact and social links**: its line reads "Help: Your contact email and social links ↗", above the box of things to fix (if there is one). Open **Commissions** in the **Commissions** section and check its line reads "Help: Commissions ↗".
7. On a phone, open `/help` on the site. The six guides are listed with a line about each. Open "Commissions, your about page, the home page and your links" and go to "If something goes wrong": the table scrolls sideways in its own box, and the page itself doesn't.
8. In "Editing your art, writing, shop and commissions", in the **Commissions** section, choose "Open or close commissions": it goes to that section of the Site settings help page, on the site.

## What you found

Every guide is one choice away from anywhere in the editor, each item's line goes to the part about it, help always opens in a new tab, and the help pages read well on a phone.
